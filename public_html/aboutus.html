Aruljothi PipeWorks

Since 1996, Our Co., has been a trusted manufacturer and trader of high-quality concrete and cement products, including pipes, kerb stones, RCC benches, and fencing poles. Based in Karur, Tamil Nadu, we pride ourselves on strict quality control, advanced infrastructure, and a dedicated team of 150+ employees.

With an in-house quality testing center, every product undergoes rigorous checks to meet superior standards. Guided by Mr. Kavin’s expertise, we have successfully served 400+ clients, staying ahead in the industry with innovation and excellence.
